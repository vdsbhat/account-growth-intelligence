# AI contribution record

## Initial prototype

The project topic draws on the portfolio owner's described background in account intelligence, segmentation, campaign analytics, and whitespace analysis. The owner agreed to the account–product prioritization scope. An AI assistant proposed the initial methodology and generated the synthetic data generator, SQL, Python implementation, dashboard, documentation, and automated tests.

The portfolio owner has reviewed the dashboard, requested clearer terminology and interactive filters, and identified excessive near-perfect priority scores. The owner requested a more realistic demo; the assistant diagnosed the generous assumptions and implemented scoring version 2. This is evidence of the owner's critical review and direction, not a claim that the owner independently coded or validated every calculation.

## AI inside the application

In a further review, the owner requested more realistic engagement and complementary-ownership components. The assistant implemented age-adjusted engagement and a synthetic product-adoption field, and tested the calculations. Those added assumptions remain explicitly documented and have not been validated with real usage or conversion outcomes.

There is no live language-model integration. Account briefs are deterministic templates filled with calculated facts. They are labeled accordingly in the dashboard. No external account data is sent to an AI service.
